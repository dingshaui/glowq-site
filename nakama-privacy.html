Privacy Policy — Nakama Match Last updated: July 20, 2026 Nakama Match ("the app") is a memory card game. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains what data the app handles. Data we collect None. Nakama Match does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no login, and no server. Data stored on your device The app saves your game progress (best scores, best moves, and unlocked levels) and your music on/off preference locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app. Third parties The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Nothing is sent to any external service. Children The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

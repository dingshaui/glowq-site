GlowQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

What We Collect

When you use GlowQ, we collect:

  • Account info: Your name and email from Apple or Google sign-in
  • Photos: Selfies you upload for beauty scoring
  • Scores: Your beauty scores and feedback history

How We Use Your Data

  • Photos are analyzed by AI to generate your beauty scores
  • Scores are stored to show your history and trends
  • Anonymized scores are used for the global leaderboard (your name is never shown to other users)

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Google Cloud in the United States.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Photos are sent to an AI service for analysis and are not retained after processing.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at glowq.app@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions? Email us at glowq.app@gmail.com.

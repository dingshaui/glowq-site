FAQ

How does scoring work?
GlowQ uses AI to analyze your selfie across five beauty dimensions: Glow, Aura, Balance, Harmony, and Structure. Each dimension is scored 0-100.

Why are my scores different each time?
Lighting, expression, angle, and skin condition all affect your scores. For the most consistent results, take selfies in natural light with a neutral expression.

How do I delete my account?
Email us at glowq.app@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and all associated data.

Is my data private?
Yes. Your name is never shown to other users on the leaderboard. See our Privacy Policy for details.
